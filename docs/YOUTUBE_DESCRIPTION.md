## Description
What is actually inside a star? In this 8-minute 3D journey we dive from the Sun's corona, through the boiling convection zone, across the tachocline, into the radiative zone where a single photon takes tens of thousands of years to escape — and down to the core, where hydrogen fuses into helium. Then we zoom out to a 25-solar-mass giant and watch its onion-layered interior collapse into a supernova.

Everything is simulated in real time with Three.js / WebGL and rendered on GitHub Actions. Numbers on screen come from NASA, ESA and university astrophysics sources (see below).
